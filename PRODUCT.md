# Misty — product truth

**What it is:** a private Mac assistant for one person (v1: built for its author, a student). It reads his mail, calendar, WhatsApp and iMessage, compiles a memory wiki, and turns what needs him into short cards. Later it will be sold as a subscription.

**What makes it special:** it remembers (memory, later always-on) and it does the work on the Mac (Stream: hands, eyes and a visible cursor for any AI model).

**Surfaces:** menu bar popover (status, count, pause); main window: Suggestions (the home), Priorities, Graph (memory map), Settings; status strip (engine state, build, spend).

**Core loop:** a card appears → Misty prepares it in the background (event, reminder, reply draft, or a Stream task) → Isaac taps Go → it is done, or a draft waits for him to send. Send, delete, buy and pay always need Isaac.

**User and scene:** a busy student at a MacBook between school, coursework and building things. Glances at Misty many times a day. Wants it fast, calm and serious. Dislikes cute, generic, "AI-made" UI.

**Direction (Isaac, 2026-10-23, replaces the 2026-10-21 quiet-pro direction):** playful everywhere, but modern. He wants it to sit next to Apple's own apps, Granola, Arc and Duolingo. Look (Isaac, 2026-10-23, after seeing a black version and rejecting it): white, modern, luminous. Bright white surfaces lit by the subject colour like light, a white pill that glows and breathes with the room, black primary buttons, one vivid colour per subject, fluid spring motion. Dark or black-first looks are anti-references. UI sounds come from React Bits Sound Lab (MIT + Commons Clause). During class Misty is a side panel next to Zoom or slides. The 2026-10-21 quiet-pro look (graphite, one teal accent) is now an anti-reference too.

**Direction (Isaac, 2026-10-24, replaces the 2026-10-23 white-luminous direction):** Pocket Operator. Misty looks and sounds like a pocket hardware gadget in the Teenage Engineering spirit: matte off-white chassis, silkscreen labels, loud plastic key colours (orange, blue, green, yellow), a black primary Go key, real key travel, clicks and thunks, spring motion. Chosen by Isaac from three real SwiftUI renders. Still anti-references: dark or black-first looks, the 2026-10-21 quiet-pro teal look, glowing white pills, gradient text, rounded fonts, cute or AI-made generic UI. Contract lives in .impeccable/surfaces/.

**Mode:** Operate.
